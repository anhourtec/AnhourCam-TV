# Privacy policy — AnhourCam TV

_Last updated: 2026-10-30. Applies to the AnhourCam TV app for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV._

AnhourCam TV is made by AnHourTec. **We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.**
The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no account with us.

## What the app connects to

- **BitVision (only if you sign in to BitVision).** The app talks directly to BitVision's servers
  (`bitvision.app`), the same service the official BitVision apps use, to sign in and to show your
  cameras. What BitVision does with that data is covered by BitVision's own privacy policy.
  AnhourCam TV is not affiliated with BitVision.
- **Your own IP cameras.** The app connects directly to cameras on your home network (RTSP / ONVIF)
  that you add. Their video never leaves your network because of this app.
- **Your phone or computer, on your home network.** While you have the TV's setup card open, the TV
  runs a small web page on your local network (protected by a one-time code) so you can sign in,
  edit layouts and add cameras from a phone. It stops when you close the card, after 10 minutes at
  most, and cannot be reached from the internet.

Nothing is sent to AnHourTec or to anyone else.

## What is stored on your TV

Only on the TV, inside the app's private storage, and removed when you uninstall the app:

- your layouts and the list of IP cameras you added;
- your BitVision **session** (not your password — the BitVision password is used once to sign in
  and is never stored), encrypted with the TV's hardware-backed Android Keystore;
- IP camera passwords, encrypted the same way.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts of your TV. Its only permission
is network access.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/anhourtec/AnhourCam-TV/issues. If this policy
